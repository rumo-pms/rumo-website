Rudy · AI assistant

A colleague who
never takes a day off.

Rudy knows your hotel, your procedures and your guests. Ask a question in your own language and he answers. Ask him to do something and he proposes it, you approve. (And yes, in Polish too if that is what it takes.)

He sits in the same rumo as you: he sees your reservations, your rates, your guests and your own handbook. And he can do no more than the person talking to him.

Rudy knows a lot. Not everything.
But really a lot.

Rudy is no wizard and he does not pretend to be one. What is yours to decide, he leaves to you. But the rest is not a short list: creating reservations, drawing up quotes, filling in group lists, searching your handbook, importing Excel, drafting replies.

What he does

The daily handling.

The routine is what he takes off your hands: the hundreds of small actions that click your day to pieces. What can be undone he does straight away; for what cannot, he waits for your yes.

  • Looking up what is going on: free rooms, arrivals, the balance on a booking
  • Reading a request you paste in and turning it into a reservation
  • Explaining how to do something in rumo, because he knows the manual

Under a proposal you see what would actually be booked, at the price from your own rates. So a proposal he discussed but never carried out does not quietly reach your guest.

What he leaves alone

Honest about what you decide.

Some things he deliberately does not do. Not because he cannot, but because they are decisions with a tail.

  • No credit notes or refunds on his own. He flags it with a proposal
  • No pricing policy. He points at where you change a rate, he does not decide
  • No internal team chat, and no hotel other than yours
How you ask him

Just English.
No syntax.

Type or say what you want, in the language you think in. Rudy looks at your context (which hotel, which date, which guest) and proposes an action. Unsure? He asks. Cannot do something? He says so, without dancing around it.

You askWhat Rudy does
"Paste the request in and say: turn this into a reservation"Reads the mail, puts the booking together, fills in the guest. You approve.
"Combien de chambres sont libres demain ?"Réponse en français. No language button to tick, he switches along with the question.
"What was my revenue last week per channel?"Shows a table. No action needed.
"Write a reply about parking, in English"Drafts the answer for the guest chat. Reception sends it.
"What does our handbook say about the no-show procedure?"Quotes the right passage from your handbook, with the source.
"Give guest Janssens a refund of €80"He does not do that himself. Sends a notification to your receptionist with the proposal.
