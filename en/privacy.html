Privacy

How we handle your data.

In plain language. What we collect, why, who we share it with, and what you can do about it.

Version
17 May 2026
GDPR ePrivacy Belgian law
Last updated · 17 May 2026

1. Who is responsible?

A hotel booking involves two parties: the hotel where you stay, and rumo, the software that hotel works with. We each have a different role.

The hotel (the hotel you booked with) is the controller. They decide which data is needed, how long it is kept, and who it is shared with outside rumo.

Rumo (Rumo BV, Ghent, Belgium) is the processor. We supply the software, host the servers and make sure everything runs. We do nothing with your data except what the hotel asks us to do, laid down in a data processing agreement.

Do you have a question about your booking or your data? Contact the hotel first. They have the full picture and can help you fastest. We step in where needed.

2. What we know about you

When you book a room

  • Name, email address, phone number and address
  • The dates of your stay, the room type and any remarks you leave
  • Payment details, which go straight to our payment partner Stripe; we never see your full card number ourselves

On arrival and during your stay

  • Identity details from your identity card or passport (compulsory under the Royal Decree of 27 April 2007 for hotel guests in Belgium)
  • The signature on your registration card, if the hotel takes it digitally
  • What you consume in the hotel and the invoicing that goes with it

What we do not do

  • No advertising pixels, no Google Analytics, no Facebook pixel, no "personalised advertising". On our website a cookieless European counter records how many visitors come by; it builds no profile (see Cookies)
  • No sale or passing on of your data to third parties beyond what is listed under "processors" below
  • No automated profiling with legal consequences for you

3. Why we have it

The law (article 6 GDPR) requires a valid reason for every piece of data. For us that comes down to:

  • Performance of your booking contract. Name, contact details, dates of stay. Without those we simply cannot give you a room.
  • Legal obligation. ID details (Royal Decree of 27 April 2007), invoicing and VAT records (Belgian VAT Code, 7 years), declarations to the police services where compulsory.
  • Legitimate interest. Securing the systems, fraud prevention, handling complaints. Always weighed against your interests.
  • Consent. Marketing mails or newsletters from the hotel, only if you explicitly say yes, and you can always withdraw that again.

4. Who we share with

Rumo brings in a number of other companies to make the service work. Those are our sub-processors. All with a data processing agreement, all inside the EU or under a valid transfer mechanism.

  • Scaleway (FR), hosting of the servers and the database
  • Stripe (IE/US), payment processing, card storage
  • Channex (UK), connection with Booking.com, Expedia, Airbnb and other distribution channels
  • Brevo (FR), transactional emails (confirmations, reminders)
  • Mistral (FR, via Scaleway), AI features when your hotel uses them (e.g. document classification or an internal assistant for staff). Never used to train on guest data.

In addition, the hotel can connect other systems to rumo that we do not choose, for example an accounting package, a till system or a key system. Those are listed in their own privacy statement.

5. How long we keep it

  • Invoices and accounting records: 7 years (Belgian VAT Code)
  • Guest register with identity details: at least until the end of the following calendar year (Royal Decree of 27 April 2007), in practice often 5 years
  • Booking history and correspondence: as long as useful for the hotel relationship, then deleted or anonymised
  • Tokenised payment card: until the booking is fully settled, then removed, unless you explicitly keep it for a future stay
  • Marketing consent: until you withdraw it

6. What you can do

The GDPR gives you six concrete rights. Sounds grand; mainly it means you have a say.

  • Access: ask which data we hold about you
  • Rectification: have mistakes corrected
  • Erasure: ask for everything to be deleted, except what we are legally required to keep
  • Restriction: ask for processing to be paused
  • Portability: get a copy in a reusable format
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interest

Ask the hotel you booked with first. They can arrange it for you in rumo. If that does not work within a reasonable time, or if it concerns rumo itself (our software infrastructure, for instance), mail privacy@rumo.eu.

Not satisfied? You can always lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Authority in Brussels.
