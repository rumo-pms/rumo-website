What drives us

We build this because we run into it ourselves, every week.

rumo is built by a hotelier and an engineer. That hotelier runs two hotels in Ghent together with his brother. Everything below was an irritation there first, and a piece of software only afterwards.

Hotel Chamade & Carlton
Ghent, 73 rooms
No advice on how to run your hotel Just the things that keep coming back

Four things that keep coming back.

Not the big strategic questions. Just the things that cost you half an hour every week and make you think: surely this should not still be a thing.

"Who blocked that room, and why?"
What we do about it Every change carries a name and a time. Not to catch anyone out, but so the question is answered after one click instead of after three phone calls.
A guest is at the desk, the receptionist does not know the answer, so she calls you.
What we do about it Your own procedures live in rumo, and Rudy searches them with the question as it was asked. He points at the article the answer came from, so nothing gets made up.
It is the third of the month and another declaration has to go out that nobody quite knows the contents of.
What we do about it The accommodation statistics, the city tax and the police registration are built from what your guests already filled in. Filing stays your signature; the counting does not.
You only notice an inspection has expired at the moment somebody asks for it.
What we do about it Your installations, certificates and inspection dates sit together, with a warning thirty days ahead. Enough time to still book an appointment.

And what we are not here to do.

Because a fair amount gets promised in this trade, and most of it is about your profession rather than about our software.

We are not here to tell you how to run a hotel.
Why not You have probably been doing that longer than we have. What we know is the administrative layer around it: declarations, inspections, commissions, connections. We dug into that because we had to work it out ourselves.
We do not have an answer to everything.
Why not Some things we do differently than you do, and sometimes you are right. Where rumo does not do something, we would rather say so straight away than have you find out two weeks in.
We are not reinventing the wheel.
Why not A calendar, an invoice and a channel manager have been around for thirty years. The difference is not in what it does, but in how many clicks and how much explaining it takes.

What we worked out along the way.

Part of that administrative layer had to be untangled before we could build rumo. It is written down, including the parts where none of our buttons come into it. In Dutch, for now.
