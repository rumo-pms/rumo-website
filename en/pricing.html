How does it work?

rumo Pay processes payments directly to your account. Guests pay via a secure payment page, from a payment request, the booking engine, or the guest portal.

Payouts happen automatically to your bank account. You keep track of everything in rumo: which guest paid, when, and for which reservation.

In case of a chargeback (disputed payment), the disputed amount + a one-time dispute fee of €15 is temporarily held on your account. Win the dispute and everything is released.

No monthly fees. No minimum volumes. No contracts. You only pay when a guest pays.
