Prevention & wellbeing Add-on · €29/month

Fire prevention. Legionella. First aid.
Under control at last.

Every hotelier knows the feeling: somewhere in a cupboard there is a folder with inspections, checklists and safety plans. Nobody is quite sure whether it is all still valid. Until an inspector turns up.

Because a hotel is also a building, and that building has its own paperwork. Fifteen installations, each with its own company, its own interval and its own certificate. That is not a technical problem but a document problem.

Installations

Every installation has its own clock.

Per installation you record what it is, until when the certificate is valid, who maintains it and who inspects it. Those last two are deliberately separate names, because that is the question you ask a year from now.

  • You do not set the status; it follows from the expiry date
  • An installation without a date gets no status. That is not the same as being in order
  • rumo does not know the statutory interval. What you fill in is what it works with

On time

You see it before anyone asks.

An inspection that expires next week cannot be arranged next week. First an electrician comes to repair what the previous inspection noted, and only then the inspection body. Two diaries, two waiting times.

That is why rumo warns thirty, fourteen, seven and one day ahead. The bell shows live what needs attention: expiring inspections, open tasks, an evacuation drill older than a year.

  • Those warnings come in the app, not in your mailbox. Someone has to open rumo
  • The bell is for managers and admins

The file

A file that is ready and waiting.

The certificates, reports, permits and plans themselves, up to 25 MB per file. With, per document, who wrote it out and until when it is valid.

  • Versions instead of overwriting: the old one is archived, the chain stays complete
  • Per document you choose who may see it. Rudy sticks to that same rule
  • Send him a stack of certificates and he lines them up, but he never files them himself

Registers and checklists

Checklists that actually happen.

Alongside the technical side runs a second track: incidents, first aid, psychosocial facts, evacuation drills, legionella, chemical products, and who came to work in the building.

The difference between a task and a checklist is the proof. A task only remembers when it was last done. A checklist leaves a trail: who carried it out, what came out per point, and a signature. That becomes a PDF with your documents by itself.

  • A contractor signs in with a QR code, without a rumo login
  • Those visitor registrations are deleted after two years. If you want to keep something, export it in time
