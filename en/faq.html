General

Do I need to install anything?

Nope. Just open your browser and go. No installation, no IT department needed. Works on your computer, tablet and phone. As long as your browser isn't from 2003, you're good.

I only have 6 rooms. Isn't rumo overkill?

Quite the opposite. rumo is built specifically for hotels with 5 to 55 rooms. The big systems can be overkill for you. rumo gives you what you need, without all the stuff you don't need.

Does rumo only work for hotels, or also for B&Bs?

Both. Whether you run a 6-room B&B or a 35-room boutique hotel, rumo scales with you. The interface is designed so that even an owner who does everything themselves can work with it smoothly.

Getting started

Can I import my existing reservations?

Yes. We help you with the switch, whether you're coming from another PMS or from a dog-eared notebook. We make sure your data ends up neatly in rumo.

How long does it take to get started?

Depends on how complex your setup is, but most hotels are up and running within a few days. Creating rooms, connecting channels, going through settings. We do that together with you.

Is there an annual contract?

No. You do start with an initial 6-month period, paid upfront. That's fair for both of us: you give rumo a real chance, we make sure everything runs. After that it's month to month, cancel whenever you want. No fine print, no notice period.

Invoicing & Peppol

How does Peppol work in rumo?

You create an invoice for a company and download it as PDF or UBL 2.1. That download is included. With the Peppol upgrade, rumo also sends it automatically over the Peppol network in UBL 2.1 format. Your accountant receives everything neatly, including credit notes and proformas.

What if my guest is an individual?

Then a PDF invoice goes out by email. Peppol is only required for B2B. rumo automatically detects which type of invoice it should be. You don't have to think about it.

Can my accountant have access?

Sure. (Phew.) Invoices are automatically forwarded to Exact Online, Yuki or Octopus. Your accountant gets everything neatly, without you having to do a thing.

Can I export invoices for my accounting?

Absolutely. Individually or in bulk: CSV, Excel, PDF or UBL 2.1 XML. There's also an accounting export with VAT breakdown per month or quarter. Your accountant is going to love you.

Reports & profiles

What reports are included in rumo?

Occupancy rate, revenue per channel, ADR, RevPAR and a central invoice overview. Plus an aging report that shows who still needs to pay. Everything exportable to CSV, Excel or PDF. No more spreadsheet acrobatics.

Are guest profiles created automatically?

Yes. Every guest, booker and company automatically gets a profile based on reservation data. Stay history, contact details, VAT numbers and Peppol IDs. Everything is tracked without you having to do anything extra.

Does rumo automatically send emails to guests?

If you want it to, yes. Confirmation email on booking, pre-check-in instructions a few days before arrival, invoice at check-out. Everything configurable per template, in your guest's language. You can also turn it off if you prefer to email them yourself.

Booking engine & channels

What is the booking engine?

A booking widget you place on your own website. Guests choose their room and dates, pay online, and the reservation appears directly in rumo. No commission, no middleman. You save the 12 to 21% you'd otherwise pay to an OTA.

Does rumo work with Booking.com and Airbnb?

Yes. Via the built-in channel manager, prices and availability are synced in real time. New booking on Booking.com? It's in rumo instantly. No more copy-pasting.

What are allotments?

Rooms you reserve in advance for a partner, like a travel agency or conference organizer. You give them a certain number of rooms per night at an agreed price, with a cut-off date. After that date, unused rooms are released back to you. Handy for groups and contracts.

What payment methods does rumo Pay support?

Bancontact, iDEAL, Visa and Mastercard. Pre-authorizations, automatically charging Booking.com VCCs. Local payment methods are included.

Does rumo work with my current accounting software?

Direct integration with Exact Online, Yuki and Octopus. Different package? Works via export. Don't see yours listed? Drop us a message, we'll see what's possible.

Can I manage multiple hotels in rumo?

Yes. Each hotel has its own configuration, rooms, rates and reservations. You switch between hotels with one click. Ideal if you manage multiple properties.

Daily operations

Can I book multiple rooms in one reservation?

Yes. A reservation can contain multiple rooms, each with their own check-in/check-out date, room status and guests. Handy for families or groups that book together but don't leave on the same day.

How does the guest portal work?

Each guest gets a personal link (via email). There they can fill in pre-check-in with ID details, confirm a quote, pay online or provide their credit card as a guarantee. You don't have to do anything. The link is valid until 2 weeks after check-out.

Is there a breakfast list?

Yes. The F&B module shows per day which guests have breakfast (included in the rate plan or separate). Plus minibar and restaurant charges that are automatically added to the room bill.

Can I send quotes?

Yes. Create a reservation with status "option" and send the guest a link via the guest portal. The guest can confirm the quote online. You get a notification as soon as that happens.
