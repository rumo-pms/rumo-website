Security & privacy Included · not an add-on

Who can get at your guest list?

Most software assumes that whoever is inside belongs there. rumo does not. You log in with your finger, you see no guest data by default, and before money or a user moves you have to confirm again that it is you.

If something does go wrong, the letter to your guests and the notification to the authorities are ready before you have properly started looking.

  • Log in with your finger
  • Alarm on unusual behaviour
  • Notification ready within 72 hours

The front door

A password alone is not a lock.

rumo works with passkeys: you confirm with your finger, your face or the code of your own device. There is nothing to look over your shoulder at and nothing to reuse.

  • Second factor compulsory for everyone, through Google or Microsoft too
  • Automatic sign-out after a while of doing nothing. Half an hour is the maximum
  • Dormant accounts switch off by themselves after 90 days. So last summer's intern no longer has a key

If someone does get in

The real work starts after that.

A stolen account looks from the outside like an ordinary colleague. So rumo does not look at who logs in, but at what that person does.

  • Reading alarm when unusually many guests are opened in a short time. Where the limit lies is set by us, not by the hotel
  • Decoys: whoever sits guessing reservation numbers runs into a made-up reservation. Open it and you give yourself away
  • Finger on the button for money and for people: refunds, roles, exports

Guest data

Housekeeping does not need phone numbers.

Email addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth: those are shielded by default. Not only on your screen, but on the way there too.

  • Openable per field, and every opening is logged with a name and a time
  • Housekeeping and F&B are not sent them at all
  • Mailing a guest is done by the server. Your screen never needs that address

And what about us?

Can we look inside your hotel?

Fair question, because a supplier who can quietly get at everything is exactly what you should be afraid of. The answer: only if you ask, and only to look.

  • Per question, not per hotel. The permission hangs on one support conversation and ends with it
  • Look, do not touch. We cannot save, change or delete anything
  • Nobody's account borrowed. Your log shows who at our end looked, by name
  • Withdrawing works on the very next click, not tomorrow

If it does go wrong

72 hours. That is the whole margin.

If something goes wrong with guest data, the law gives you 72 hours to report it. That clock starts the moment you know, not when you have finished working it out.

  • The list: rumo lines up who was staying with you in that period
  • The warning: one button mails those guests, each in their own language, from your hotel
  • The bank: a letter on your letterhead for anyone who has lost money
  • The notification: the article 33 form, filled in with what the file holds

The 72-hour clock is there the whole time, counting down.

What we do not claim.

A page about security with only good news on it is not a page about security.

  • We do not hang an ISO certificate on the wall. We do not have one. Do you need one from your insurer or your chain? Say so and we will talk about it, rather than buying a logo for it.
  • We cannot promise that nobody on your team will ever click a bad link again. Nobody can. What we do is make sure it yields as little as possible afterwards.
  • The alarms are tripwires, not locks. They stop nothing; they make noise and write things down. That is deliberate: a lock set too strictly simply blocks someone on a busy Saturday.
  • And what you store with us stays yours. If you ever leave, you take everything with you in an export. Without having to ring anyone for it.
