Housekeeping

Who does which room today?

One list a day. A room goes dirty by itself on a check-in, on a check-out and every morning again. Housekeeping sets it clean.

  • Your calendar shows per room whether it is still dirty
  • Works on a tablet in the corridor, and there is a printable list
  • A cot or a late departure lands on the card by itself

Need more than clean and dirty? Then there is Advanced Housekeeping.

Breakfast

By morning the kitchen already knows what it is in for.

No till, just the list the kitchen needs. The count follows the night: whoever arrives today has breakfast tomorrow, whoever leaves tomorrow still has breakfast that morning.

  • Allergies sit with the room, not in a separate notebook
  • Tick off who has been, and your colleague sees it straight away
  • Someone extra at the table? The kitchen adds them itself, and it lands on their bill

Allotments

Holding rooms for a group, without losing track of them.

You hold rooms back for a group, usually at a negotiated rate. That is not a block in your calendar, it is a promise with an expiry date.

  • What you hold comes off your availability, on Booking.com too
  • Pickup shows how many of the agreed rooms were actually used
  • On the cut-off, whatever is unbooked goes back on sale by itself
  • Invoice per booking, or all at once to the company

Rapporten

Your figures are already there. You do not have to go and fetch them.

Pick a period and you get a dozen or so reports on those same days. No exports you have to knead in Excel first.

  • Revenue, average room rate, RevPAR and occupancy, per room type and per channel
  • Pace: are you ahead of last year, or behind?
  • Debtors, nationality, online check-in, breakfast

And separately: the police register, the city tax and the government statistics. Built from what your guest filled in. Filing stays your signature; counting does not.
