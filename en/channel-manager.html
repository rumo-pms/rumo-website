Channel manager

One change,
every OTA.

You change a price in rumo and within seconds it is on Booking, Airbnb, Expedia and the rest. A booking comes in and it is on your calendar straight away. One subscription with us, no separate bill for the connection.

What you set here applies to your whole house, including your own booking page. It has to: that is the one channel with no channel manager in front of it.

Back and forth

Everything they need to know.

Out to the channels go your rates, your availability and your restrictions. Back come the bookings and the cancellations. Usually within a handful of seconds.

  • Restrictions belong to a room type and a rate plan. Close one and the others stay open
  • A bulk edit is prepared, not sent: it only leaves when you save
  • Cancellations and price changes after booking you do in the OTA's extranet

Auto close

If your desk is closed, the night is closed.

Set what time your reception closes. rumo then puts that night on stop-sell by itself, on every channel and on your own booking page at the same time. There is nothing to press.

It closes only that night. Tomorrow stays on sale, so a guest can still book ahead while there is nobody at the desk.

  • A guest sees it as sold out, not as closed. On purpose
  • Departing that morning is still possible, because it costs you nothing that night
  • Your reception can still take a booking by hand

More than sync

What else rumo does with the OTAs.

Sending rates and availability is the beginning. Behind the scenes rumo settles a good deal more that you would otherwise have to sort out by hand at Booking or Airbnb.

Booking.com

Invalid card? Booking sorts it out.

If a guest card turns out to be dead, rumo reports that back to Booking.com. Booking asks the guest for a new card and gives you the right to cancel if it does not work out.

Inbox + Rudy

Guest messages from the OTAs, in one place.

Messages from Booking.com and Airbnb arrive in rumo, Rudy drafts the answer and reception sends it. Expedia does not take part in this. You take this connection separately; the rest of this page is in your package.

Virtual cards

Card handling per channel, automatically.

Verify, pre-authorise or charge automatically, set per channel. A virtual card from Booking often only becomes valid shortly before arrival, so rumo does not try too early and retries by itself.

Net revenue

The real commission, per booking.

rumo reads the commission amount that comes along with every booking. So your net revenue per reservation is right, instead of guesswork with an average percentage.

Optional add-on

Smart Pricing, built in.

Prices that move with your occupancy, in steps you set yourself and pushed straight to your channels. No external tool needed.

Deduplication

No double imports.

If a channel sends the same booking twice, rumo recognises it and holds the duplicate back.

Status panel

See at a glance whether the sync is running.

Last booking in, last change sent, and what went wrong. If something is stuck, you push it again by hand.
