And beyond that

Everything you expect from hotel software. And maybe a little more.

Included in your plan

On from day one, at no extra charge.

F&B

Counts, allergies, walk-ins and printed lists. An overview of your covers, not a till.

Messages & Inbox

Internal chat between your team and a shared inbox for your guests. Rudy drafts the answer, you send it.

rumo pay

Payment links, card charging and pre-authorisations. Bancontact, Visa and Apple Pay. Lands on the invoice by itself.

Invoicing & Peppol

Invoices, credit notes and proformas with the right VAT. Download as PDF or as UBL 2.1.

Reports

Occupancy, ADR, RevPAR and revenue per channel. Export to CSV, Excel or PDF.

Belgian compliance

City tax, police registration and government statistics, straight from what your guest filled in. Set it up once.

Allotments

Fixed room blocks for tour operators or corporate accounts, with pickup tracking and central billing.

Maintenance

Broken lock on room 12? Anyone reports it with a photo. You see what is waiting, what is running and what is coming.

Add on separately

Modules you switch on or off by the month. What they cost →

Prevention & wellbeing

Fire safety, legionella, first aid, inspections and incidents. One file instead of four folders.

Smart Pricing

Prices move with your occupancy and go straight to your channels. You set the limits, and stepping in yourself always wins.

Upsell

Guests upgrade their room or add extras before arrival, through the portal. Without work at the desk.

Advanced Housekeeping

Cleaning checklists per room and a timer that measures how long a clean really takes.

White Label

Your logo and house style on the portal, the booking engine and every email. Your guest never sees rumo.

Key management

Digital keys from Salto, tied to the reservation. The guest checks in and the door opens.

Peppol automatic

Your invoices go onto the Peppol network by themselves. No XML to download, no portal to open.

Accounting link

Your accounting package, Peppol and a digital cash book. Everything goes to your accountant by itself, through Scrada.

What rumo connects to.

Just look for your own channel or your own accounting package in the list. Open a category if you want to know what the connection actually does.

OTAs and booking platforms12 Booking.com · Airbnb · Expedia · Google Hotel Ads · Agoda · Trip.com · HRS · Check24 · eDreams · Hopper · VRBO · Getaroom Where your rooms are sold online.
Booking.comThe world's largest booking platform. Prices, availability and reservations synced in real time.
AirbnbReach travellers looking for unique stays. Perfect sync for short and longer stays.
ExpediaExpedia, Hotels.com and Vrbo in one connection. International reach for business and leisure travellers.
Google Hotel AdsDirect bookings via Google Search and Maps. Guests book directly with you.
AgodaPopular with Asian travellers. Growing fast in Europe, especially for city trips and budget hotels.
Trip.comWide reach in Asia and growing in Europe. Ideal if you want to attract international guests.
HRSFocused on business travellers and corporate clients. Strong in Germany, Benelux and Central Europe.
Check24Popular comparison site in Germany. Reach German travellers who book price-consciously.
eDreamsEuropean OTA with strong reach in Spain, France and Italy. Combines flights with hotels.
HopperMobile-first platform, popular with younger travellers. Growing channel for last-minute bookings.
VRBOVacation rentals via the Expedia network. Ideal for apartments and family rooms.
GetaroomGlobal distribution via various booking channels. Extra visibility without extra work.
Tour operators and wholesalers4 Hotelbeds · Travelgate · Reconline (GDS) · Hyperguest The business circuit: travel agents, groups, GDS.
HotelbedsGlobal B2B network for travel agencies and tour operators. High volume, wide reach.
TravelgateMarketplace connecting you with hundreds of distributors and online travel agencies worldwide.
Reconline (GDS)Access to Amadeus, Sabre and Travelport. Reach business travellers and traditional travel agencies.
HyperguestDirect, commission-free B2B network. Improve your margins by working straight with travel agents.
Accounting and payments6 Exact Online · Yuki · Octopus · Scrada · Peppol e-facturatie · rumo Pay Where your invoices and your money go.
Exact OnlineInvoices sent automatically. VAT breakdown, credit notes and proforma invoices included.
YukiDirect connection with Yuki. Invoices and payments appear automatically in your administration.
OctopusPopular with Belgian accountants. Invoice export and VAT filing without extra work.
ScradaThe bridge to your accounting package and cash book. Scrada passes your invoices and corrections on to Exact Online, Yuki, Octopus and the rest.
Peppol e-facturatieMandatory for B2B in Belgium. Downloading as PDF or UBL 2.1 is included; sending over the Peppol network is an add-on.
rumo PayPayments built in. Bancontact, Visa, Mastercard, iDEAL. Payment links, VCCs and pre-authorisations.
Revenue management3 RoomPriceGenie · Pricelabs · Ratedock External pricing tools, if you already use one.
RoomPriceGenieAutomatic price optimisation, designed specifically for small and mid-sized hotels. Set it up and let it work.
PricelabsData-driven dynamic pricing. Analyses market data, events and competitors to optimise your rates.
RatedockCentralised rate and inventory management across all your channels. Clear and efficient.

SaltoDigital keys linked to your reservations. Check in via the app, instant room access. No more key cards.
