About us

Built by a hotelier and an engineer.

rumo isn't a product from some big software factory. It's founded by a hotelier and an engineer. One who stands behind the desk every day, and one who knows how to turn that into software.

The founders

A hotelier.
An engineer.

J

Jeffrey

Co-founder · Product & Design

Jeffrey runs two hotels in Belgium with his brother: Hotel Chamade (51 rooms) and Carlton Hotel (22 rooms), both three-star. He knows how a front desk operates, how not to get woken up for a late arrival, and he sees why most PMS systems are too complicated for what they do.

He designs rumo from what he needs every day. Not features that look good on a slide, but functionality that's actually used by real receptionists.

S

Seba

Co-founder · Technology & Architecture

Seba works in an innovation team in the tech industry and builds software that scales. He's responsible for everything under the hood: the backend, security, infrastructure, and integrations.

He makes sure rumo is fast, secure, and reliable. Multi-tenant architecture, real-time synchronization, Peppol e-invoicing, channel manager integration. The technical foundation everything runs on.

Why rumo

Hotel software from a real hotel.

Jeffrey has worked in hotels for years and missed a system that actually matched how his team works. Something fast, clear, that doesn't require three days of training. So he decided to build it himself.

Today we're building rumo: a PMS that focuses on what small and mid-sized hotels really need. No more. No less. Not 400 features where you use 12. Just software that works the way you think.

"We didn't build software for hoteliers. We built what we'd been looking for ourselves for years."

How we think

Three principles.
No more, no less.

01

Simplicity above all

If a receptionist doesn't get it in 10 seconds, it's not good enough. Every click, every screen, every label is weighed on simplicity.

02

Built for real hotels

No theoretical features. Everything in rumo is tested in a working hotel. If it doesn't work at 7 AM during a check-out rush, it doesn't belong.

03

Honest and transparent

No hidden costs. No annual contracts. No hour-long sales calls. What you see is what you get. And if rumo isn't for you, we'll tell you that too. No hard feelings.
