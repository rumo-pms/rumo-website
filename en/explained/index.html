Uitgelegd

What we had to work out ourselves.

To build this software we first had to understand how it really works. When an inspection on your building expires, how an allotment quietly drains away, why Expedia sends no commission invoice, why training a new receptionist takes just as long every time. What we learned along the way is here.

Training

You train more often, for people who are there less often.

More students and flexi-workers behind your desk, staying for less time and learning more slowly because they work less often. About what you can write down once, what you can only learn by doing, and why an practice environment starts with a look at the mail.

3 minutesStaff
 Your team

How do you know who has read the new procedure?

You change your fire procedure, and half your team has read the old version. About read confirmations tied to a version, photos as the standard, and why a changed procedure is trickier than a new one.

3 minutesStaff
 The building

Who maintains your gas boiler, and when was the last time?

A hotel is also a building, and that building has paperwork: fifteen installations, as many intervals, and four kinds of paper all called "a certificate". Why the inspection is rarely the problem and the paper afterwards almost always is.

3 minutesPrevention
 Groups and allotments

"Does this request belong to the conference, or not?"

Holding rooms for a group is a promise with an expiry date, not a block in your calendar. About pickup, the cut-off that costs you money without anyone noticing, and who pays which line of the bill.

3 minutesGroups
 OTAs and commission

Why do you get no commission invoice from Expedia?

Two bookings of a hundred euros, both at fifteen per cent commission, and still they may not sit in your books the same way. With a worked example in which both columns come out the same at the bottom.
