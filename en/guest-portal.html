Arrival

In without a key, without a desk.

Linked to your Salto locks, the guest gets their codes for the front door, the parking and the room. If they arrive at midnight, nobody has to be at the desk.

The code only comes when everything is in order. If there is still a balance outstanding or the check-in is not done, it stays locked. If it does not work out and the guest arrives today, the bell tells your reception.

Compliance

Police registration, automatically.

What the guest filled in themselves takes care of three obligations at once: the police registration, the city tax on their invoice and the government statistics.

Whoever types it in at the desk counts it over three times afterwards. Here it comes from the source. Filing stays your signature; counting does not.
