10|1. First, some framing
11|There are two kinds of data in rumo. <strong>Guest data</strong> (bookings, ID cards, invoices) belongs to the hotel; that explanation is on the <a href="privacy.html">general privacy page</a>. There the hotel is the controller and rumo the processor.
12|<strong>Staff data</strong> is something else. When you log in to rumo as a receptionist, manager or housekeeper, we are recording your account, your login history and your actions. There <strong>rumo itself is the controller</strong>. We cannot put that role with the hotel, because it is our software that makes and keeps the logs.
13|This is the explanation of that second part.
14|2. What we know about you
15|Your account
16|First name, surname and email address, used to log in and to show you in the software
17|Your role in the hotel (admin, reception, housekeeping, F&amp;B, ...), which decides what you can see and do in rumo
18|Your password (hashed, never stored in plain text) and possibly a second factor if that is switched on
19|Optionally: a profile photo you uploaded yourself
20|How and when you log in
21|For every login we record:
22|Time
23|IP address
24|Browser and operating system (the "user agent")
25|That sits in the <code style="font-family:'JetBrains Mono',monospace;font-size:0.85em;background:var(--sand);padding:0.1em 0.35em;border-radius:4px;">login_log</code> table and is needed to detect suspicious sign-ins. For instance: someone logs in at 3 a.m. from another country while you are asleep in Ghent.
26|What you do in rumo
27|<strong>Audit logs</strong> on sensitive actions: price changes, validating or charging payments, refunds, credit notes, guest registration. We keep who did what when, plus the old and the new value.
28|<strong>Staff messaging</strong>: messages you send in internal channels or DMs, with any attachments.
29|A general activity log at hotel level: who opened, changed or cancelled which reservation.
30|Support contact
31|Emails you send to <a href="mailto:hello@rumo.eu">hello@rumo.eu</a> or <a href="mailto:support@rumo.eu">support@rumo.eu</a>, plus our replies
32|Any screenshots or examples you send along to illustrate a problem
33|What we do <em>not</em> do
34|No keystroke tracking, no mouse heatmaps, no session replay
35|No profiling based on your work pattern ("this receptionist is slow")
36|No sale of employee data to third parties
37|No tracking pixels in our emails to see whether you open them
38|3. Why we have it
39|<strong>Performance of the agreement between rumo and your hotel.</strong> You have an account because the hotel gives you access as a member of staff. Without account data we cannot deliver that.
40|<strong>Legitimate interest, security and accountability.</strong> Login logs and audit logs are there for fraud prevention (someone entering suspicious refunds, say), for incident response (something odd happens and we have to be able to reconstruct it), and for the hotel itself, which has to be able to check its own staff where needed.
41|<strong>Legal obligation.</strong> Audit trails on financial actions are required by accounting legislation and for fighting fraud.
42|<strong>Consent.</strong> Product updates or newsletters from rumo itself, only if you explicitly say yes, with the right to withdraw.
43|4. Who we share with
44|We run rumo at a handful of other companies. All inside the EU or under a valid transfer mechanism. All with a data processing agreement:
45|<strong>Scaleway</strong> (FR), hosts the servers and the database your data sits on
46|<strong>Brevo</strong> (FR), sends transactional emails such as "someone has invited you", password resets, login notifications
47|<strong>Mistral</strong> (FR, via Scaleway), if your hotel uses Rudy (the AI assistant), your question to Rudy and the context of your role can go to Mistral. Not used to train the models.
48|<strong>Not</strong> at Google, not at Microsoft, not at OpenAI, not at Meta. No Sentry connection at this time. On our <em>website</em> rumo.eu there is a cookieless visitor counter (Plausible, EU-hosted). That only counts visits to the marketing pages and does not touch the software you work in: nothing about you as a member of staff passes through it. See <a href="cookies.html">Cookies</a>.
49|5. How long we keep it
50|<strong>Your account</strong>: as long as you are an active user. If your access is withdrawn by your hotel, we set the account to inactive; full deletion after 30 days unless there is a legal retention duty (financial audit trails, for instance).
51|<strong>Login logs</strong>: 90 days, then deleted automatically. Long enough to spot recent anomalies, short enough not to build years of surveillance.
52|<strong>Audit logs on financial actions</strong>: 7 years (VAT Code). No different from paper cash books.
53|<strong>Staff messages</strong>: according to the retention policy the hotel sets itself (typically 30-365 days, adjustable per hotel).
54|<strong>Support correspondence</strong>: two years, then deleted unless a matter is still running.
55|6. What you can do
56|The same six rights as any EU citizen:
57|<strong>Access</strong>: ask for a copy of what we hold about you
58|<strong>Rectification</strong>: have mistakes corrected (a typo in your name, a wrong email address)
59|<strong>Erasure</strong>: ask for everything to be deleted, except what we are legally required to keep
60|<strong>Restriction</strong>: ask for processing to be paused
61|<strong>Portability</strong>: get a copy in a reusable format (JSON/CSV)
62|<strong>Objection</strong>: object to processing based on legitimate interest
63|For account matters in rumo itself: ask your hotel administrator first. They can change a good deal for you in Settings, or delete your account.
64|For everything rumo itself keeps (login logs, support mails, etc.): send an email to <a href="mailto:privacy@rumo.eu">privacy@rumo.eu</a>. We reply within 30 days, usually sooner.
65|Not satisfied? You can always complain to the <a href="https://www.gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Data Protection Authority</a> in Brussels.
